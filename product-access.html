Overview

What Access Control does

Access Control brings readers, controllers and electronic locks together under one management screen, so you decide who can go where and see what actually happened.

It can sit alongside our building-automation work, bringing access control and HVAC into one monitored application.

Biometric and cardReaders for faces, fingerprints and cards on the same system.
Controllers and locksIntegrates controllers and electronic locks at every door.
Multi-siteManage several buildings or floors from one screen.

Features

Everything in Access Control

Biometric and card readers

Support for biometric and card readers at entry points.

Controller and lock integration

Connects door controllers and electronic locks so rules are enforced at the door.

Access logs and reports

Searchable records of every entry and exit, ready to filter and export.

Instant alerts

Be told straight away when something needs attention.

Multi-site management

Oversee several locations from a single dashboard.

Building integration

Bring access control into the same app as HVAC through BACnet and Modbus.

Common questions

Which readers and locks can you connect?

We work with biometric and card readers, controllers and electronic locks. Send us your hardware list and we will confirm what fits.

Can it manage more than one site?

Yes. Multiple sites can be managed from one place.

Can it connect with building systems?

Yes. We integrate with BACnet and Modbus systems so access and HVAC can share an app.
