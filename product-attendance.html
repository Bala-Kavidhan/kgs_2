Overview

What Employee Attendance App does

The Employee Attendance App gives every employee a simple, personal view of their working day: check in, check out, see hours, and keep track of leave.

Each customer gets a standalone installation, set up around their own people and rules rather than a shared public service.

StandaloneInstalled separately for each customer, so your data stays your own.
Geo-aware check-inCheck-in can take location into account.
Real-time dashboardEmployees see their own attendance as it happens.

Common questions

Is this the same as People360?

No. This is a standalone mobile app for attendance and leave. People360 is the full multi-tenant HR and payroll platform.

Does each customer get their own installation?

Yes. It is installed separately for each customer.

Can it be branded for our company?

Yes, we can tailor the look and settings to your organisation.
