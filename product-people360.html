Overview

What Kavidhan People360 does

People360 replaces spreadsheets, registers and disconnected tools with one system for the whole employee lifecycle: who is present, who is on leave and what each person is paid.

It is built as a multi-tenant platform, so the same installation can serve multiple organisations while keeping their people, roles and settings separate. Admins work from the web panel; employees stay connected through the mobile apps.

Multi-tenantRun several organisations or branches from one platform, each with role-based access.
Web and mobileA web admin panel for HR and mobile apps for employees.
Biometric-readyWorks with face-recognition and biometric attendance devices.

Features

Everything in Kavidhan People360

Face-recognition and biometric attendance

Capture attendance from face-recognition and biometric devices, so records come from the person, not a paper register.

Leave management

Employees request leave, managers approve it, and balances update without anyone keeping a separate sheet.

Payroll and payslips

Automate the payroll cycle and generate payslips from the attendance and leave data already in the system.

Role-based multi-tenant admin

Give HR, managers and owners exactly the access they need, across one or many organisations.

Web admin panel

Dashboards, employee records, approvals and reports in one place for the HR team.

Mobile apps

Check in, view attendance and manage requests from a phone, wherever the team is working.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it really multi-tenant?

Yes. One platform can serve several organisations, each with its own people, roles and settings.

Which biometric devices does it support?

It is designed to work with face-recognition and biometric attendance devices. Share your device model and we will confirm compatibility.

Can payroll rules be customised?

Yes. Payroll, leave and shift rules are configured to match how your organisation works.
